## FAQs

What is Royal Perks and how can Guests join?

Royal Perks is Burger King's loyalty program where members can access exclusive digital coupons, participate in monthly challenges, earn Crowns to redeem rewards, and much more! You can sign up by going to bk.com and clicking "Sign Up" or downloading the BK® App.

What are the benefits of joining Royal Perks?

As a Royal Perks Member, you will receive the ultimate Royal Treatment! Our members earn 10 points, called "Crowns," for every $1.00 USD spent on eligible transactions at participating U.S. Burger King restaurants, which can then be redeemed for eligible BK® menu items. Our members also earn 2x Crowns on all eligible transactions during their birthday month in addition to the choice of a free Whopper® Jr., Original Chicken Sandwich, Cheeseburger, 8pc. Chicken Fries, Soft Serve Cup or Cone, or seasonal item on the day of their birthday. Additional benefits include access to exclusive offers, and opportunities to earn bonus Crowns through exclusive challenges and limited-time mini games.

What if there is a problem with my Royal Perks account?

Members can contact our support team at bk.com/support if they are having any issues with their Royal Perks account, including issues related but not limited to transactions or Crowns.

Where can I find the Royal Perks Terms and Conditions

Participation in the program is subject to Royal Perks Terms and Conditions. Full terms can be found at bk.com/royalperksterms. If you no longer wish to participate in Royal Perks, please go to the opt-out link at the end of this page to request the deletion of your account and data. You may also submit your request to BK's Privacy Officer at privacy@rbi.com.

What is an offer?

An offer is a digital coupon you can redeem on the "Offers" tab in your Royal Perks account! They are only available to Royal Perks members, and must be redeemed on the BK® App or bk.com.

What is a reward?

A reward is a FREE BK® item that you can redeem using the Crowns you have earned by making purchases on your Royal Perks account! Rewards can be accessed on the "Rewards" tab in your Royal Perks account. Earn 10 Crowns for every $1 spent.

What is a badge?

A badge is a digital collectible that you can earn for hitting milestones in your Royal Perks journey! You can find them in your loyalty profile section, and will be notified via a pop up message when you navigate there.

Why don't I see Crowns in my account after I just earned them?

Crowns can take up to 24 hours to be assigned back into your account. If they have not been added to your account after the 24-hour period, please contact the BK Guest Care team at bk.com/support.

How many rewards can I redeem in one transaction?

Royal Perks allows one (1) free item redemption per transaction. There must be a 60-minute cooldown after your reward redemption before you are allowed to redeem another reward.

How many offers can I redeem in one transaction?

Royal Perks allows one (1) offer redemption per transaction. There must be a 60-minute cooldown after your offer redemption before you are allowed to redeem another offer.

Do my Crowns expire?

Crowns earned in your Account will expire on the first (1st) day of the month following the sixth (6th) month from the date such Crowns were earned. Expiration occurs early on the 1st of the month, and expiring Crowns may not be available for redemption on that day. Your last opportunity to redeem expiring Crowns is the day before the 1st of the month. For example, if you earn 50 Crowns on January 5, 2026, and 50 Crowns on January 28, 2026, those 100 Crowns will expire on August 1, 2026, and must be redeemed no later than July 31, 2026.

If you no longer wish to participate in Royal Perks, Click Here to request deletion of your account and data. You may also submit your request to BK’s Privacy Officer at privacy@rbi.com. Complete list of Tiers and Rewards is available at https://www.bk.com/royalperksterms.

Initial Guidelines 

Listen, we know you’ve probably got a lot of big ideas for the next Whopper. While we’re super eager to see what you’re cooking up, we think it’d be best we provide some guidelines. First, your Whopper entry will consist of a list of toppings you will be asked to enter. Sesame seed bun and patty selection are default elements of your Whopper Idea that can’t be removed, nor do they count as part of your entry. So, that means it doesn’t matter if you pick an Impossible TM patty or beef patty and you won’t be able to make a candy sprinkle bun (that does sound pretty good, though). 

Now, we’re sure the toppings you want to add are going to be great. But, we want to make sure you keep a couple things in mind about toppings including: 

- Toppings must be edible food items. (duh!) 
- Toppings must be written in English. 
- Toppings can’t exceed more than 25 characters. 
- Toppings cannot be a “prohibited allergen” defined as Peanuts and crustacean shellfish. 
- Examples of peanuts include, but are not limited to things like actual peanuts & peanut butter. 
- Examples of crustacean shellfish include things like shrimp, crab, lobster, and crayfish. 
- The order of the toppings is not relevant to the judging of a Whopper idea. 
- Toppings do not have to be a single ingredient, but rather can be a mixture of ingredients which is typically treated as a single item. For example, salsa, marinara sauce, and pesto all include multiple ingredients but are treated and available for sale as a single food item. 
- Include specifics. If the Topping comes in different varieties and/or flavors, the variety should be listed. For example, blue cheese, granny smith apple, red onion. 
- If the intent is to use a Topping more than once, such as two slices of American cheese, Entrant you should list “American cheese” two times to reflect two (2) separate Toppings. We’ll let you use branded Toppings, but keep in mind that Burger King might have to obtain a license, so we reserve the right to substitute such Topping with a generic version for use in the Limited Time Offer Whopper® sandwiches. 
- Keep in mind Toppings will be subject to a feasibility score that considers the practicality of using the Topping on a hamburger prepared at a Burger King restaurant. So, consider the availability, the cost of the Topping, and whether it’s something our Burger King restaurants can easily store, prepare and use on a Whopper sandwich. 

These are just a few of the guidelines, so please see Contest Rules for full details and additional guidelines on Toppings.
