## REWARDS FAQ

How to use Chipotle Rewards? 

To use Chipotle Rewards, simply sign up for the Chipotle rewards program through the app or website. Once enrolled, you’ll earn reward points on every order—10 points per $1 spent. You can then redeem those points for free food like a Chipotle free entrée or even a Chipotle free burrito. Members also unlock rewards like free chips and guac, exclusive Chipotle promo codes, and access to surprise drops, making it one of the best free rewards programs available. College students also have access to Chipotle U Rewards.

Do points and rewards expire? 

No—Chipotle Rewards points don’t expire as long as you make at least one qualifying purchase per year. This makes Chipotle’s system different from many rewards programs or e-rewards platforms that expire points quickly. It gives you more flexibility to save up your reward points for bigger rewards like a Free Entrée or other items in the Chipotle rewards exchange.

Does Chipotle do birthday rewards? 

Yes! Chipotle offers Chipotle birthday rewards as part of its Chipotle Rewards program. Members can choose one birthday reward during their birthday month—pick from chips, guac, queso, or a drink. It’s one of the many rewards that puts Chipotle among the places that give birthday freebies and offer free birthday food. 

Can you use multiple rewards at Chipotle? 

Chipotle typically allows only one reward per order, meaning rewards, Chipotle coupons, and Chipotle promo codes cannot be combined.

How to get free Chipotle? 

There are several ways to get free Chipotle. The easiest is to join the Chipotle rewards program and earn points toward rewards like a Chipotle free burrito or Chipotle free entrée. New members may also receive a sign up get free food offer like free chips and guac. Additional opportunities include Chipotle promo codes, surprise drops, and limited-time offers, making it one of the top options for first order free food and ongoing rewards.

Does Chipotle give Military Discounts? 

Chipotle does not offer a traditional, always-on military discount, but they do occasionally run special promotions or appreciation events for military members. In addition, joining Chipotle Rewards ensures access to ongoing value through reward points, free Chipotle offers, and exclusive rewards that are available to all members.
